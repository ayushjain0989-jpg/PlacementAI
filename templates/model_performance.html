Why Compare Multiple Models?

PlacementAI evaluates multiple machine learning algorithms rather than relying on a single model. This helps identify the model that provides the strongest predictive performance for the dataset.

Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 Score and ROC-AUC are measured on a held-out 20% test split. The model that is actually saved and used is chosen on the mean F1 score across 5-fold cross-validation instead, because a single split of a 401-row dataset moves by several points depending on which rows happen to land in the test set.
